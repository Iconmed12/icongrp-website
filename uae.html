Practice One

Bid Management

Most bids are not rejected on quality. They are removed from evaluation before anyone reaches the quality section. We fix eligibility and documentation first, then write.

Eligibility Screening

Checked against your actual position before you commit time to a bid.

Document Control

Licences, certificates, audited accounts and signatory authority kept current and ready to submit.

Supplier Registration

Registration and prequalification with the platforms and entities relevant to your sector.

Bid Writing

Technical proposals, method statements and quality responses, once eligibility is confirmed.

Submission Management

Compliance matrix, internal deadlines, format checks, upload and clarifications.

English and Arabic

Produced in house, including where documents need translation for submission or attestation.

Before You Bid

What We Check First

Every one of these can remove a bid from evaluation. We confirm them all before you invest time in writing.

Trade licence validity and age
Licensed activity matches the tender scope
Chamber of commerce membership
Tax registration certificate
VAT position
Audited financial statements and turnover threshold
Insurance certificates and limits
ISO and sector certifications
Authorised signatory and power of attorney
Attestation and legalisation of foreign documents
Past experience evidence and completion certificates
Submission format and deadline requirements

If any of these are wrong, the rest of the bid does not matter.
