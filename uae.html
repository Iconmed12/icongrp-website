The Problem

Your Bid Never Got Read

Most bids are not rejected on quality. They are removed from evaluation before anyone reaches the quality section.

Eligibility not checked first

Licence age, sector classification, turnover thresholds and experience requirements ruled you out before you started writing.

Documents out of date or missing

Expired trade licence, missing tax registration certificate, lapsed insurance, no attested copies, wrong signatory.

Format and submission failures

Wrong file structure, unsigned pages, incomplete forms, late upload.

Before You Bid

What We Check Before You Bid

Every one of these can remove a bid from evaluation. We confirm them all before you invest time in writing.

Trade licence validity and age
Licensed activity matches the tender scope
Chamber of commerce membership
Tax registration certificate
VAT position
Audited financial statements and turnover threshold
Insurance certificates and limits
ISO and sector certifications
Authorised signatory and power of attorney
Attestation and legalisation of foreign documents
Past experience evidence and completion certificates
Submission format and deadline requirements

If any of these are wrong, the rest of the bid does not matter.
