Our Capabilities

What do we deliver in the DRC?

Construction

Need full construction project management?

ICONGRP manages construction projects from inception to completion, including planning, coordination, delivery and oversight, with a focus on quality, safety and local operating requirements.

Training

Need training to build capability?

ICONGRP provides training to help organisations and teams meet international operating, documentation and compliance standards, with practical, context-specific programmes for the DRC.

Compliance

Need supply chain checks and compliance support?

ICONGRP supports traceability, due diligence and responsible sourcing, providing site verification and supply chain checks for Congolese exporters, international buyers and partners.
