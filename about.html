About ICONGRP

Built with purpose.
Growing with ambition.

ICONGRP is an international group built around people, opportunity and long-term impact. From our beginnings in the United Kingdom to our presence across Africa and the Middle East, our growth has been shaped by a commitment to doing things properly, building strong partnerships and creating lasting value.

Our Story
Our Story

From one idea to an
international group.

ICONGRP began with a clear ambition: to build an organisation capable of creating opportunity through specialist services, strong relationships and responsible delivery.

What began in the United Kingdom has developed into a wider international group, with operations and partnerships extending into the Democratic Republic of Congo and the United Arab Emirates.

Our growth has not been about becoming bigger for the sake of size. It has been about building capability, entering markets where we can add real value and creating an organisation designed for the long term.
